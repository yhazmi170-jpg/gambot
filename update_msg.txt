NEW:
`v stocks` -- buy & sell stocks, market drifts every hour
`v bank shark` -- take a loan, pay it back
`v hatch` -- open pet eggs (eggs drop while hunting!)
`v trade` -- trade animals with other players
`v heist <fee>` -- start a heist, crew joins, roll for the payout

`v vault deposit/withdraw` -- shared server vault
`v achievements` -- unlock badges for milestones
`v blackmarket` -- rotating limited deals, refresh every 6h
`v dex` -- see which species you own vs missing
