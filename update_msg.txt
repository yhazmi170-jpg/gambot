v1.7.2 — Battle Pass & Checklists

NEW
- `v pass` — **Seasonal Battle Pass**: earn XP from hunting, battling, hatching, sacrificing, giving, working, gambling, wins, and quest/bounty/checklist completions. Level up a free track + a premium track (25 seals) and claim coins, gems, and seals at every tier (25 tiers, 14-day season). Sub-commands: `v pass buy`, `v pass claim`, `v pass top`, `v pass rewards`.
- `v checklist` — multi-task daily + weekly checklists (hunt / battle / hatch / gamble / gems). Finish all tasks for coins + collectible **seals** (`v checklist claim` for daily, `v checklist weekly claim` for weekly).
- `v snailgarden <bet>` — plant garden rows one at a time; the payout multiplier grows ~1.25x per row while the failure chance climbs. Cash out with the Sell button before a row fails (button-based, like mines).

NOTES
- Seals are a new collectible currency: earn them from daily/weekly checklist rewards and from premium pass tiers (every 5th tier). Spend 25 seals to unlock the premium pass track for the season.
- The pass tracks your level + progress, shows the season timer, and the top command shows who's ranked highest this season.
