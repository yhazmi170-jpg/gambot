v1.7.1 — Bounties & Fixes

NEW
- `v bounty create @player <goal> <amount>` — player-funded PvP bounty: you put up the prize, first to reach the duel-win goal against the other takes the whole pot. `v bounty list` for the board, `v bounty info <id>`, `v bounty cancel <id>` (before any duels are fought). Duel wins now show bounty progress live.
- `v wheel <amount>` — spin the wheel of fortune, multipliers up to a x10 jackpot.
- `v streak` — daily logon streak bonus that scales to day 7 (miss 48h and it resets).
- `v loan take|shark|pay` — borrow coins from the bank (30% interest, up to 2x your balance+bank) or hit the loan shark (50%, up to 2M). Winnings auto-repay your loan.
- `Aovo events #channel` — put random server events in their own channel (falls back to update channel).
- More random events (15 now): Hot Streak, Gem Bonanza, Hatch Madness, Essence Flood, Double Payday + more.

FIXES
- Mines: board was missing the bottom-right tile (4x4 but 15 buttons) — all 16 tiles now show + a dedicated cash-out row; unplayed games refund your bet instead of silently eating it (5 min window).
- Giveaways: no more silent no-winner endings — giveaways are saved to the DB and a sweep always draws, pays and announces the winner, even after a restart.
- `v zooshop buy flower garden` now works (multi-word item names).
- PvP battles 'your team is empty' bug fixed.

NOTE: the owner boot-DM only fires once per version now, so no more spam DMs on every restart.
