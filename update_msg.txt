v1.8.0 — What's New

- ⚔️ NEW: Pet Weapons! Teams can now equip weapons that boost ATK/DEF and add special battle effects
  - `v weaponcrate buy` (15k coins) → `v weaponcrate open` for a random weapon
  - `v weapon list` / `v weapon equip #<weaponId> #<animalId>` / `v weapon upgrade #<weaponId>`
  - 8 weapon types: Great Sword (splash), Poison Dagger (DoT), Flame Staff (burn), Vampiric Staff (lifesteal), Healing Staff (team heal), Defender Aegis (DEF + taunt), Bow, Rune of Power
  - 7 rarities, common → fabled, each with a quality % that scales your stats
  - Weapon crates also drop from `v battle` wins and `v hunt` (~8% per animal)
  - Equipped weapons show up in `v team`

Enjoy!
