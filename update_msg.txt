v1.7.0 — The Pet Evolution Update 🐾

NEW COMMANDS
- `v crate <common|premium|mythic>` — loot boxes for random pets, with a pity counter that guarantees a top-rarity drop
- `v freebet [amount]` — claim 500 free house coins a day and gamble them. lose = house money, win = real coins
- `v raid <stake>` — fight the server boss. stake adds to the pot, damage splits it on the kill
- `v clan <create|join|leave|info|deposit|withdraw|kick|top>` — server-wide clans with a shared treasury
- `v auction <id> <min> [hours]` + `v bid <id> <amount>` — sell pets to the highest bidder
- `v plot buy|upgrade|claim` — own land that pays coin income every hour
- `v evolve <id>` — evolve a pet to the next rarity (costs essence)
- `v feed <id>` — feed a pet for a 2-hour battle buff
- `v fuse <id1> <id2>` — merge two pets into one stronger pet
- `v family` — view your family tree (partner, parents, kids)
- `v zooshop` — buy zoo decorations that show at the top of your zoo

NEW GAMEPLAY:
- Shiny pets ✨ (rare, 2x sell price) and a new MYTHIC tier 👑 (Odin, Tiamat, Bahamut, Cthulhu, Godzilla)
- 20+ new pet species including Otter, Penguin, Capybara, Jaguar, Sphinx and more
- Pet personalities: Brave, Chill, Eager, Lucky, Calm — each gives a small battle edge
- Random server events: Gold Rush (wins +25%), Lucky Hour (gems x2), Egg Mania (eggs x2), Essence Surge (sacrifice x2)
- Loss streak protection: lose 5+ in a row and losses start to refund extra
- Vaults now earn 0.4% interest per hour
- Married bonus: couples get +10% on daily, weekly and work
- Pet achievements: first blood, level milestones, evolutions, fusion, shinies and more

`v zoo` shows shiny badges and your decorations; `v animal` shows personality, fed status and pet achievements. `v dex` now tracks the full 60+ collection including mythic.
