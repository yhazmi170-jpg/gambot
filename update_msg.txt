v2.1.0 — Snail Garden Progression

- 🐌 Snail garden got a real progression system
  - `v garden pet` — pick a 🐌 snail, 🐸 frog, 🪱 worm, 🐱 cat, 🐰 bunny, 🦔 hedgehog, 🦊 fox, 🦉 owl or 🐉 dragon to run your garden (dragon is the hardest + juiciest)
  - `v gardenshop` — 5 permanent upgrades, unlocked by garden level: better 🌱 soil (+growth on rows 5+), ripe 🧬 seeds (+profit on rows 5+), a 🕸 safety net (survive a failed deep row), comfy 👢 boots (+1 row cap up to 13 rows), the golden 🚿 can (+2% profit on rows 5+, forever)
  - Garden XP + levels — every run earns XP (`v garden` shows your level bar), levels unlock pets + upgrades, level-ups pay bonus coins
  - Events on deep rows (12%) — Light Rain (+growth), Sunshine (+profit), Bugs (+fail) and the silent Bedrock
  - survivability is strictly better than profit: your deep-row chance of dying stays safe, and the math is audited so NO runner or upgrade combo is ever a guaranteed win — the house keeps its edge
  - `v garden pet` / `v garden shop` / `v gardenbuy <upgrade>` aliases work too
  - untouch a garden before it grows and it's a full refund — xp/levels/achievements only start when you actually plant a row

🎉 `v giveaway` got a whole lot bigger
- New syntax: `v giveaway <time> <prize> <winners> <split|full>`
  - `v giveaway 24h 100m 10 split` → 100m divided across 10 winners = 10m each
  - `v giveaway 24h 100m 10 full` → each of the 10 winners gets the full 100m
  - `v giveaway 24h 100m 10` → defaults to split
  - `v giveaway 24h 100m` → the old 1-winner syntax still works exactly as before
- Up to 50 winners. Winners are drawn uniquely — nobody wins twice.
- Host is charged correctly: split = the pot; full = prize × winners.
- If fewer people enter than winner slots: split distributes the full pot, full refunds the unused slots to the host. Split remainders are refunded too.

📭 Prizes now land in the inbox
- Every winner gets their prize as an inbox delivery (`v inbox` or the Claim All button) instead of instant balance.
- Inbox shows giveaway prizes with a 🎉 marker.
- The final giveaway embed now lists the winner(s) and reminds them to claim.
